# BAD — Official Primary Brand Mark

**Approved:** November 5, 2026

**Brand:** BAD ERA
**Primary outward-facing logo:** `BAD` (uppercase, white, bold geometric sans-serif wordmark), exactly as supplied by the owner and used in approved streetwear and field-gear boards.

## Files

- `BAD-primary-white-transparent.png`: primary white mark on transparent background for dark fabrics, product pages, gear, patches and packaging.
- `BAD-primary-black-transparent.png`: same geometry in black for light backgrounds.
- `BAD-primary-white-on-black-square.png`: the *unchanged owner-supplied image*, ideal as a square social/app presentation image.

## Non-negotiables

1. This exact owner-selected letterform is the master; do not substitute a font, redraw, reshape, slant, stretch, or add effects. The transparent assets were derived directly from the supplied image, preserving the glyph silhouettes.
2. Monochrome black/white is default. Metallic silver, camouflage, embroidering, and patches are *applications* of the same mark, not replacement identities.
3. Keep adequate clear space around the mark (recommended: at least 1/4 the logo letter height on each side).
4. Maintain aspect ratio. Ensure legibility on small embroidered/engraved items; test with the actual vendor before production.
5. `BAD ERA` is the brand name. `BAD` is the primary consumer-facing wordmark. A full-name lockup and a secondary icon can be developed later, but should not silently change this master.
6. Concept-board images are illustrative and not accurate production artwork. The original owner-supplied black-and-white artwork is the reference authority.
7. The legacy BE logo remains present in the existing BAD ERA 2.0 repository until a deliberate site-wide implementation updates components, assets, docs, and test fixtures. No GitHub changes were made by this package.

## Production note

These are high-quality PNG raster files, not a mathematically editable vector master. Before laser engraving, large-format signage, screenprinting separations, or legal trademark filing, commission a designer to build and compare a faithful vector drawing against the owner-supplied master. Trademark availability and ownership have not been assessed.
